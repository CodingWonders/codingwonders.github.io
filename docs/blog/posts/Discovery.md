# What's Next for "Discovery" 
 
Discovering new levels of system administration. 
 
<!-- more --> 
 
With DISMTools 0.8.2 getting closer to its release, we begin to wonder once again: "What's next?", "What will bring us closer to the future of system administration?", and "What will be the next big thing?". And thus, we begin to **discover** a new realm of possibilities.

The next DISMTools Series, the 0.9 Series, will be codenamed "Discovery" for 2 reasons:

1. It allows us to discover new ways to innovate in the system administration space
2. It is a direct reference to Daft Punk's "Discovery" <!-- ONE MORE TIME -->

These features have been under consideration for a couple of months, and I want to make sure they are perfect for this Series. But, showing you everything when completed, given the magnitude of the content, can get overwhelming. As such, I have decided that I am going to publish a set of Sneak Peeks showing you what is planned for this new Series.

Stay tuned!
